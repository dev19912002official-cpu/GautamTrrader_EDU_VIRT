About

Why this channel exists

I'm Gautam Kumar Jha. I started out trading the same way most people do — undersized on research, oversized on positions, and reactive instead of deliberate. The losses taught me more than the wins ever did.

This channel exists to shorten that learning curve for others: honest breakdowns of what the market is doing, why it matters, and how to build a process that survives being wrong sometimes — because everyone is, sometimes.

No hype. No guaranteed calls. Just the work, shown honestly.
